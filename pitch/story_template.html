Rebuild the city

To look out from every floor, I rebuilt the city.

The scans gave me streets, trees and rooftops. I traced 36 sightlines from each floor, one every 10°, out to 1.5 km in 5 m steps. Each line showed where buildings and trees blocked the view. Then I climbed.

Follow the evidence

I price the flat from the evidence closest to it.

That became my modelling rule. Start with the flat's own adverts, then its building, then its suburb. Keep the view adjustment inside the building, where the comparison makes sense.

Start with its own adverts

I move past asking rents to the valuation date using a same-flat repeat-rent index.

Check how old they are

I set own history aside after 36 months, so an old advert cannot anchor the estimate indefinitely.

Let neighbouring flats count equally

I use same-building comparables with one flat, one vote. Repeated adverts for one flat cannot dominate.

Adjust views within the building

I apply a small LiDAR view adjustment within the building, using the view measured for each floor.

Widen to the suburb

When closer evidence runs out, I use suburb and bedroom comparisons.
